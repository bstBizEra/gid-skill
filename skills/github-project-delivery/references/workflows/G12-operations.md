# G12 — Operations

**Trigger:** Measure delivery/runtime outcomes, respond to an incident, or plan operational improvement.
**Inputs:** Indicator definitions, time window/timezone, denominator, telemetry coverage, incident evidence, owners.
**Capability assumptions:** Telemetry and incident tooling have defined coverage; unavailable sources remain unknown.
**Actions:** Define measures; report coverage and exclusions; assess incidents; mitigate/repair under authority; verify recovery; feed measured improvements into planning.
**Output:** Performance report, incident record, or linked improvement tasks.
**Exit and recovery:** If telemetry or owner data is missing, report the gap rather than infer an outcome; retain incident timeline and next owner action.
**Authority limit:** Repository Insights alone cannot prove customer, runtime, or business outcomes.
